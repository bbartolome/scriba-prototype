Planner's small decisions (recorded here, not in `plans/DECISIONS.md`):

- P1. Names and files: `ShellSkeleton`, selector `app-skeleton`, in `apps/web/src/app/shell/skeleton.ts`; `AboutPanel`, selector `app-about-panel`, in `apps/web/src/app/shell/about-panel.ts`. The skeleton is a small component of its own. Angular Material has no skeleton, and its progress spinner is a spinner, not a placeholder.
- P2. Shortcut keys: Alt+`[` (`event.code` `BracketLeft`) goes to the previous nav entry, and Alt+`]` (`BracketRight`) goes to the next. The handler matches `event.code`, the physical key, so Option+[ and Option+] should work on a Mac layout (assumption A2, checked by hand in step 17). It needs Alt and no Ctrl, Meta or Shift. Reason: CodeMirror's default keymap binds Alt+ArrowUp, Alt+ArrowDown, Alt+ArrowLeft, Alt+ArrowRight, Alt+l, Mod+[ and Mod+] (`node_modules/@codemirror/commands/dist/index.js` lines 1797 to 1810), and the editors use that keymap (`apps/web/src/app/shell/markdown-editor.ts` line 365; `apps/web/src/app/features/tool-registry/tool-editor.ts` line 163). Alt+[ and Alt+] are bound in neither.
- P3. The shortcut does nothing when its event target is an `input`, a `textarea`, a `select` or a `contenteditable` element, so typing and the editor are never taken over. Its cycle is the nav entries shown at the current device class (`AppNav.items`, `apps/web/src/app/shell/app-nav.ts` lines 190 to 197), in nav order, wrapping at both ends. When the current URL path matches no entry, next goes to the first entry and previous to the last.
- P4. After a shortcut navigation, focus moves to the link of the entry it opened. SHL-19 owns the wider focus-order pass and may change this.
- P5. Empty nav: when no entry of the route table has `data.nav`, at any depth, the nav shows the text "No features registered." Device-class filtering alone shows nothing, as it does now.
- P6. The About panel reads the version, the revision and the health each time it is created, with no cache. A failed read shows its message in `role="alert"`, as `landing.ts` does (lines 77 to 82).
- P7. Skeleton markup: a `span` with `role="status"` that holds visually hidden text "Loading" and one `aria-hidden="true"` bar per line. The pulse animation runs only inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`. The bar colour is the Material token `--mat-sys-surface-container-highest`, which Angular Material defines (`node_modules/@angular/material/core/tokens/_classes.scss`).
- P8. Landing: the skeleton replaces the word `loading` only. The loaded text is unchanged, because `apps/web/src/app/shell/landing.spec.ts` line 107 checks "Platform version: 1.2.3".
- P9. Switch page: the skeleton replaces the `<h2>` while the project list read is pending.
- P10. The shortcut text for the About panel, if OP-2 chooses it: "Alt+[ and Alt+]: previous and next nav entry (Option on a Mac)" and "Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on a Mac) in the editor: save now".
