## 1. Decisions this plan follows

Each item gives its source. FACT means checked by the planner (how is in section 7a), or a recorded decision or unit text. ASSUMED means a choice made here because the sources are silent (section 7b).

1. **Module and wiring.** All code goes in the existing `SetupAuthModule` (`apps/api/src/modules/setup-auth/setup-auth.module.ts`). No composition-root change: `COMPOSED_MODULES` already holds `SetupAuthModule`. Source: `app.module.ts` lines 14 to 18; unit line 15. FACT.
2. **Routes.** `GET /api/setup-auth/status` (`SETUP_STATUS_ROUTE`) and `POST /api/setup-auth/continue` (`SETUP_CONTINUE_ROUTE`), both from the contract. Continue is a command, so POST (`HTTP_METHOD_CONVENTION.command`). Source: unit lines 15 and 16; `packages/contracts/src/setup-auth/routes.ts` lines 4 and 5; `packages/contracts/src/platform/http.ts`. FACT.
3. **Authentication of both routes.** Without a valid session cookie the route answers 401 with code `AUTH_REQUIRED` in Platform's base error shape. Reasons: SAU-04 makes every route that is not public default-deny (`plans/SAU-04.md`, decisions 2 and 3: the public set is the routes marked `@Public()` and `/healthz`, and status and continue are neither); SAU-06 shows a 401 as the sign-in notice (SAU-06 line 17). The check is also done in the controller through `SetupCookieSigner.verify`, so the routes are correct whether SAU-04 is merged first or not. Sources: `SETUP_AUTH_ERROR_CODES.REQUIRED` = `AUTH_REQUIRED` and `SETUP_AUTH_ERROR_STATUS` = 401 (`packages/contracts/src/setup-auth/errors.ts`). The sources: FACT. The duplicate check in the controller: ASSUMED (item 14).
4. **Status body.** `setupStatusSchema`: `{steps, complete, compose}` with five steps and `complete`. Source: `packages/contracts/src/setup-auth/status.ts` lines 4 to 10 (step keys) and line 34 (schema); unit line 15. FACT.
5. **`access`.** `done` in every 200 response, because a 200 is only given to an authenticated caller (item 3). It is derived when read, not stored by the status GET, which must be side-effect free. Source: unit lines 15 and 20; `plans/SAU-03.md` decision 7 (the handshake changes no server state). The derivation: ASSUMED.
6. **The other steps.** Read from the stored `setupAuth.steps` (all `pending` in M1, because only Access exists in this phase). Source: unit line 15; `setup-auth.section.ts` (default `pending`, line 8 onward). FACT.
7. **Compose status.** Always `{state: 'unknown', expected: EXPECTED_COMPOSE_VERSION}`, with no `actual`. `expected` is the real value, from `apps/api/src/modules/platform/compose-version.ts` and `deploy/compose-version`. The state stays `unknown` until SAU-10 replaces it. Source: unit line 15 ("placeholder `unknown` until SAU-10"); `composeVersionStatusSchema` (line 27, `actual` optional). Values: FACT. Always `unknown` in M1: ASSUMED.
8. **Continue response.** 200 with the status body after the write. The unit does not name the response, so the body is the one that the status GET returns (SAU-06 needs only the 200 and the cookie). `@HttpCode(200)` is explicit, because Nest's default for `@Post` is 201 (ASSUMED from Nest's documented default). Echo's POST sets `@HttpCode(202)` (`events/echo.controller.ts` line 16). The status 200: ASSUMED.
9. **What Continue writes.** `access` becomes `done`. Every other step that is `pending` becomes `skipped`. Steps that are already `done` or `skipped` are kept. `complete` becomes `true`. Source: unit line 16 (remaining pending steps skipped; complete true); the `access` value: ASSUMED, because the caller is authenticated and item 5 says access is done.
10. **Writes are field-level merges of the `setupAuth` section.** `SettingsService.write` replaces the whole section (`settings.service.ts` line 64). So the merge is read-merge-write in `SetupStatusService`: take the raw stored section, replace `steps` and `complete`, keep `preferences`, `allowlist` and any other key, and call `write('setupAuth', merged)`. Other top-level sections are kept by `write` (it rewrites `doc[key]` only). Sources: unit line 17; SAU-02 plan item 15 and open point 7. Behaviour of `write`: FACT. The placement of the merge: ASSUMED.
11. **Side-effect-free GET.** The status GET calls `SettingsService.read` only. `read` (`settings.service.ts` line 58) parses the file and writes nothing (`settings.file.ts` `readSettingsFile`). It sets no cookie and emits no event. Source: unit line 22; module line 43 (no SSE). FACT.

A `ZodError` must not be thrown, because the filter maps it to 400 (its `ZodError` branch, line 52). Source: `platform-exception.filter.ts`. FACT for the mapping; the choice of a plain Error: ASSUMED.
